The tool I built

Gaziyav is a free, open-source video editor. In the Mac app, AI edits a real timeline: type “remove the pauses, add captions, cut it to 30 seconds” and get clips you can still adjust by hand. On any other computer, the browser edition lets you edit by hand. The videos above were made in it.
